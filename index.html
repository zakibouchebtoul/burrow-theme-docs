Getting started

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes, find Burrow and click Customize.
  2. Open Theme settings (gear icon) and upload your logo, choose your colors and fonts.
  3. Create two menus in Online Store → Navigation: main-menu for the header and footer for the footer. Nested menu items appear as drop-down navigation.
  4. On the home page, choose a collection for the Best sellers and Product slider sections, and add images to the hero and other image sections.
  5. Create an About page and assign the page.about template, and a Contact page with the page.contact template.
Burrow doesn't include demo images. Sections show placeholders until you choose an image, so upload your own photos in Content → Files or directly from each image setting.

Header and footer

Header

Choose the main menu and the customer account menu, float the header over the page, make it sticky, hide it while scrolling down, and show search. The account icon uses Shopify's account component, which lets customers sign in and reach their orders without leaving the store. The Announcement bar rotates short messages above the header.

Footer

Add Menu, Text and Newsletter blocks. Footer settings control the background image, social icons, Follow on Shop, the country/region and language selectors, and payment icons. Selectors appear when your store sells in more than one country or language (set these up in Settings → Markets and Settings → Languages).

Product page

The product information panel is built from blocks you can reorder: vendor, title, price, buy buttons, description, text, share, Custom Liquid and app blocks.

  • Media: images, Shopify videos, YouTube/Vimeo videos and 3D models with “View in your space”. Selecting a variant shows its image.
  • Variant options: color and image swatches appear automatically when your product options use swatches.
  • Buy buttons block: quantity selector, dynamic checkout buttons, gift card recipient form, pickup availability, Shop Pay Installments and subscription options (selling plans) from your subscription app.
  • Price: shows sale and compare-at prices, unit prices and whether tax is included.
  • Recommendations: the product template includes a Complementary products section (set these in the Search and Discovery app) and a Related products section.

Collections and search

Collection and search pages support filtering by availability, price, product type, vendor and variant options, plus sorting. Choose which filters appear in the Search and Discovery app. The collection header shows the collection title, description and image. Search shows products, articles and pages, and the header search shows instant results as you type.

Cart

The cart drawer shows line items, quantity controls, discounts, subscription details, unit prices, an optional free delivery progress bar and suggested products. The cart page adds order notes. Set the free delivery amount in the Cart drawer section.

Motion and accessibility

Scroll reveals, parallax, card tilt, magnetic buttons and the custom cursor are optional and can be switched off in Theme settings → Motion. All animations turn off for visitors who have reduced motion enabled on their device. Every interactive element works with a keyboard and shows a visible focus state.

FAQ

Why do I see gray placeholder images?

Burrow doesn't ship with demo images. Choose an image in each section's image setting to replace the placeholder.

How do I add drop-down menus?

In Online Store → Navigation, drag menu items under another item in your main menu. Burrow supports up to three levels.

Where do I change filters?

Install and open Shopify's free Search and Discovery app, then choose the filters you want under Filters.

How do I show subscriptions?

Install a subscription app that uses Shopify selling plans. Purchase options then appear automatically in the buy buttons block.

The country or language selector doesn't appear.

Selectors only show when your store has more than one market or language, and when they're enabled in the footer settings.

Can I turn off the animations?

Yes. Go to Theme settings → Motion and switch off the effects you don't want, or turn off all animations at once.

How do I change the colors of one section only?

Select the section, then use its Background color and Text color pickers. Cards inside many sections have their own color pickers too.

How do I add app content to a product page?

In the product template, click Add block in Product information and choose your app's block, or use a Custom Liquid block.
